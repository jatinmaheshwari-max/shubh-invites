STEP 7 · META ADSAds that bring WhatsApp chats

The best ad type for you is Click-to-WhatsApp: people tap the ad and a WhatsApp chat with you opens, with no website or form in between. One ad runs on Facebook and Instagram.

A. Set up the campaign (Meta Ads Manager)

  1. Go to adsmanager.facebook.com and select your Libesole page's ad account. Add a payment method (UPI or card).
  2. Click + Create → objective Engagement → Continue.
  3. Campaign name: Libesole – WhatsApp – Test 1.
  4. Ad set → Conversion location: Messaging apps → WhatsApp. Pick your number +91 6398252681.
  5. Budget: Daily ₹300. Run for 7 days as a test (about ₹2,100 total).
  6. Audience (see B below).
  7. Placements: keep Advantage+ placements ON (Facebook, Instagram, Stories, Reels).
  8. Ad → Single image or video. Upload the square image, and for Stories/Reels choose the 9:16 version via Edit → Customise by placement.
  9. Paste the Primary text, Headline and Description from C below. Call to action: Send WhatsApp message.
  10. Message template: choose "Get quotes" or create your own greeting (paste the WhatsApp greeting from Step 4) and add quick questions: "I want a video invite", "I want a wedding website", "Show me prices".
  11. Click Publish. Meta reviews the ad (usually within a few hours).

D. Reading your results

What you seeWhat to do
Cost per conversation under ₹40–60Good. Keep it running; after a week, raise the budget by 20–30% every 3–4 days.
Cost per conversation above ₹100 after 3 daysSwitch that ad off and try a different design or headline.
Many chats but few ordersThe ads work; improve replies. Answer within 5 minutes, send 2–3 sample designs, share prices clearly and follow up the next day.
Few chats at allWiden the location or age range, or try the video ad.
Keep ads honest. Don't use numbers such as "15,000+ happy customers" in ads unless they're true for Libesole. Meta can reject ads with misleading claims, and customers lose trust. Also avoid tiny text on images and "before/after" style claims.
Track every client in your Libesole Leads Google Sheet: add a column "Source" (Facebook ad / Instagram / Website / Referral) so you know which channel brings paying customers.

STEP 8 · ₹0 BUDGETFree ways to get clients

  1. Invite all your Facebook friends to like the page (page → ⋯ → Invite friends), and share Post 1 on your personal profile and WhatsApp Status.
  2. Join local wedding groups on Facebook (search "[your city] wedding", "wedding vendors [city]"). Read each group's rules and share your work only where allowed. Helpful answers get more clients than spam.
  3. Partner with other vendors: photographers, mehendi artists, decorators and makeup artists meet couples every day. Offer them a referral commission or a free invite for their own family.
  4. Show every finished invite (with the client's permission). Real work is your best advertisement. Post it as a Reel and tag the couple.
  5. Ask every happy client for a recommendation on your Facebook page and a Google review. Send the /thanks quick reply.
  6. Link Instagram: page settings → Linked accounts → Instagram. Post the same content to both.
  7. WhatsApp Status daily: post one design or Reel every day; friends and relatives often forward it to families with upcoming weddings.

Your 15-minute daily routine

  • ☀️ Morning: reply to every message from the night (within the first hour).
  • 📱 Afternoon: post one design on WhatsApp Status and Instagram Stories.
  • 🌙 Evening (7–9 PM): publish the day's Facebook post or Reel; reply to comments.
  • 📊 Once a week: check the Leads sheet and Ads Manager; follow up with every "New" lead.
