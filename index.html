GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, Answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Message us on WhatsApp.

How will I receive my invitation?

Video invitations are delivered as an MP4 file, invitation cards as high-quality images or PDF, and wedding websites as a personal link. All of them can be shared directly on WhatsApp, Instagram or email.

Can I make changes to the design?

Yes. We share a preview before final delivery so you can request changes to text, photos, colours and music.

How long does it take?

It depends on the design and the number of events. Tell us your event date and we'll confirm the delivery time before we start.

Can the invitation be in Hindi or another language?

Yes. We create invitations in English, Hindi and regional languages such as Marathi, Tamil, Telugu and Bengali, or a mix of languages.

What details do I need to share?

Names, event dates and times, venues, a few photos (optional) and any wording or style you have in mind. We'll guide you through the rest.
